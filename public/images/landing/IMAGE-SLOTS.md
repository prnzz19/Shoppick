# SHOPPICK landing-page reference assets

The exact supplied mockup assets are copied to `public/images/landing/reference/`.
The full-section promo, collection, hero, and CTA images already include the
reference text and button artwork, so the Blade view uses real links over the
surfaces without rendering duplicate labels.

## Asset mapping

- `hero_full_section.png` — hero visual surface
- `logo_header.png` — header and footer logo
- `promo_electronics_full.png` — Electronics promo card
- `promo_home_living_full.png` — Home & Living promo card
- `promo_fashion_full.png` — Fashion promo card
- `product_new_01_earbuds.png` through `product_new_06_desk_lamp.png` — Newly Dropped product visuals
- `collection_kitchen_full.png` — Kitchen Essentials collection
- `collection_beauty_full.png` — Beauty & Personal Care collection
- `collection_tech_full.png` — Tech Gadgets collection
- `collection_home_decor_full.png` — Home Decor collection
- `product_featured_01_backpack.png` through `product_featured_06_smart_watch.png` — Featured product visuals
- `cta_banner_full.png` — final SHOPPICK CTA
- `reference_full_mockup.png` — supplied visual specification

The product cards keep their real database product names, prices, links, cart
actions, Buy Now actions, and wishlist controls while using the supplied visual
references for their image surfaces.
