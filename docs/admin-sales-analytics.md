2. **Source reused:** `ShopSalesReportService` and `MarketplaceSalesReportService`, also used by marketplace and per-shop Sales Reports. 3. **Qualifying status:** `seller_orders.status = completed`; revenue is the stored `seller_orders.seller_total`. Parent-order totals are never added to these sales metrics. A multi-vendor purchase contributes one completed order per qualifying seller order, matching Sales Reports.
4. **Sales date and exclusions:** `seller_orders.completed_at`, with inclusive selected calendar dates implemented as an exclusive next-day boundary. Cancelled and other noncompleted seller orders contribute no sales. There is no seller-order `failed`, `returned`, or `refunded` status in the current status list. Existing reports do not independently join payment/refund records or subtract refunds; this implementation preserves that rule, including completed seller orders whose parent payment status differs. Soft-deleted shops are excluded; archived/suspended shops remain in reporting. Legacy completed rows without a completion date appear in All Time totals only, with a chart explanation.
5. **Dashboard UI:** Sales Performance replaces the old seven-day bar chart. Existing buyer/seller/application, recent-order, stock-alert, and parent-order operational sections remain. The old Revenue card now explicitly shows source-aligned Net Sales (All Time).
6. **Dashboard KPIs:** Net sales, completed seller orders, item units, and net sales per completed seller order, with previous-period changes. Default is Last 7 Days. Today, This Week, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, and This Month auto-update the section.
7. **Dashboard chart:** Responsive teal SVG line/area chart, subtle grid, native point tooltips and keyboard focus. Missing daily/monthly buckets are filled with actual zero activity. Daily buckets are used through 90 days, monthly thereafter. No chart dependency was installed.

13. **Sales Over Time:** Uses the same grouped net source as the KPI cards, including proportional category filtering. No dated activity and zero activity have separate readable states.
14. **Shop Performance:** Sales, completed orders, units, share, seller name, and previous-period sales trend; SQL sorting by Highest Sales, Most Orders, Highest Growth, or Lowest Sales; ten shops per page. 15. **Category Performance:** Top twenty web rows, all grouped categories in exports. Stored vendor net totals are allocated across items proportionally to positive item totals; zero-value orders fall back to positive quantities and then equal shares. Missing item/product/category records remain in an unallocated category. No per-item rounding changes aggregate totals. Distinct order counts can overlap across categories. Historical classification uses the available current product/category relationship, including soft-deleted products; no historical category snapshot is fabricated.
16. **Product Performance:** Top ten analytics products, top five Dashboard products, ranked by attributed net sales. Product/shop identity comes from seller-order history. Units and distinct order counts come from qualifying order items, never lifetime `sold_count`. Previous-only grouped activity remains available for comparison. Product rows do not represent the unallocated category or every product in the marketplace.
17. **Order statuses:** Every actual seller-order status has count, scope percentage, previous count, and absolute change. Completed rows use completion dates; other statuses use placement dates, matching existing operational report filtering. These are current status snapshots, not status-transition events or a conversion funnel.
18. **Cancellations:** Current and prior count, absolute change, current and prior scope rate. Cancelled rows do not contribute completed sales. No cancellation reasons were fabricated.
19. **No-sales diagnostics:** Total count and up to ten shops with no qualifying orders; total current-catalog products without qualifying completed items, up to ten rows, and exact current out-of-stock count. Stock is a current snapshot. Deleted products are not counted as current inventory.
20. **Diagnostic rules:** Sales decline greater than 20% gets Needs Attention; 10–20% gets Watch; other variations get Normal. Zero baseline gets explicit baseline wording. Additional observations identify changes in completed orders, cancellations, selling-shop counts, largest category/shop absolute sales decrease, and leading-shop share. Largest declining shop includes its previous/current orders and cancellation counts. Largest decreases are calculated across all grouped results, not just visible rows.
21. **Causation:** Insights describe observed changes during the same period. Cancellation and inventory text explicitly avoid asserting why sales changed. No guessed recommendations, simulated statistics, cart/view funnel, or causal attribution was added.
